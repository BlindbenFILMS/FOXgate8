# MERU SOUND PROMPTS · batch 1 (vehicles, skate park, weather) · 22 files

Paste each prompt into Runway. Save each file with the **exact name** shown, as MP3, into **audio/meru/**.
They replace the synth sounds; I wire them in once they are uploaded.

## RULES FOR EVERY FILE (many play at once, so keep them clean)

- **Mono**, 44.1 kHz, MP3 192 kbps.
- **Dry**: no reverb, no echo, no music, no voices. The game adds distance + walls itself.
- **Same loudness** for all: about -16 LUFS, peaks no higher than -3 dB. (Don't spend credits on levelling or trimming; the game does it at load.)
- **No silence** at the start. Tails under 0.3 s unless the prompt says otherwise.
- **LOOPS** must loop **seamlessly**: same sound at the end as at the start, no fade, no click.
- Keep **low bass light** on loops (heavy bass turns to mud when layered).
- **2 versions** where asked: two short takes that differ slightly (the game picks one at random).
