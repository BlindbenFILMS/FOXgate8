22 SUPER MOVES
Land hits to fill the SUPER meter. Double-tap the stick (Q on a keyboard): time stops for 3 s. Flick two directions, then press 1 (melee) or 2 (ranged). Every super turns to the closest opponent first. Wrong input = time returns, meter kept. ↔ = left or right, either side counts.
01 + 02 = STARTING SUPERS (IN GAME)
