How it works

Four steps

50 images can be triggered by 1 QR code

  1. Open the link

    Scan the code by the Art Alive enabled image. It opens in your phone's browser.

  2. Center your camera on the art

    Art Alive finds the Art Alive enabled image on its own.

  3. Watch, listen and play

    The Art Alive enabled image moves, tells its story, and can even turn graphics into playable games.

  4. Create your own

    Upload your own image to auto generate or create amazing animations in the Art Alive Creator Studio: add motion, sound, a timeline, or even create a game based on and framed inside your image.
