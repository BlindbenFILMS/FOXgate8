# Creature review: what would make each one better

One or two concrete upgrades per family, from going back through how each is built. Pick what you like and I'll do those first.

## Gaya (purple)
- **Crystal Crab:** claws are blunt boxes; give them a serrated inner edge and a "clack" spark when they snap. Add a little sideways scuttle shuffle in idle.
- **Crystal Golem family:** still blocky; round the body slabs like the Gate Guards, and give the Wardens a cape of crystal shards that hangs and swings.
- **Shardthrower:** reads thin from far away; add a glowing crystal crest and a shard "quiver" on its back that empties as it throws.
- **The Painted Giant:** paint is flat stripes; paint swirls and handprints would read as "painted". Let it splash paint when hurt.

## Gate Guards
- **Sentry / Charger / Warden:** now rounded; next, give each world's skin one signature detail (Sink drips, Furnace smoke vents, Hive wings, Gate gold crest, Fort sandbags), so you can tell worlds apart at a glance.
- **Charger:** add a ram plough and dust trail on the charge.

## Jidda
- **Corsairs:** add a parrot on the Captain's shoulder, gold earrings, and a boarding-axe variant for the Cutlass. Drowned ones could drip water.
- **Flamingo:** add a splash ring around their legs in the shallows and a big wing-flap honk on the charge.
- **Sand Serpent:** head is a box; a rounder skull with a frill that flares in the wind-up.

## Kufa
- **Scarab:** horn and pincers are tiny; bigger horn, shell shine, and a little sand plume when it pops up.
- **Spitter:** add a cracked clay texture look and bigger sac veins that glow as it swells.
- **Husk:** loose linen strips that flutter, and a sand trickle from its joints.
- **Dust Devil:** add debris (a bucket, a sign, a sandal) caught in the spin.
- **Wyrm:** pale body is plain; add ribbed segments and the convoy crates rattling.
- **Warden of the Siphon:** steam puffs from the chimney in idle and a whistle on the wind-up.

## Luxor
- **Slagback:** crust chunks are similar sizes; mix big and small, and drip lava from the seams.
- **Ember:** a little flame flicker on top and sparks trailing as it bounces.
- **Ventmaw:** smoke column from the mouth in idle; teeth around the rim.
- **Ashwalker:** a tattered ash cloak and footprints that steam out.
- **The First Cinder:** ember particles rising from the heart all the time.
- **Stealth Fox:** a visible ripple/refraction edge when cloaked instead of plain transparency.

## Nebo
- **Hornet:** bigger eyes, striped legs, and a buzz that dopplers as it dives.
- **Hive Queen:** a royal cape of wings and a honeycomb throne.
- **Thornback / Rootwalker:** leaves and flowers on the back, roots that dig into the ground when it stops.
- **The Deadfall:** birds or a nest in its head; moss.
- **Sporelings:** spotted caps with gills under them and puff clouds on every hop.
- **Garden Mole:** big digging claws and dirt flying; glasses on the Molewarden.
- **Shrike:** a long tattered coat and a lamplighter's hat.

## Ur
- **Reed Runner / Tall One / Spitter:** lab-scar stitches, and collars that blink a little light.
- **Wrapped Attendants:** linen ends that trail and flutter; sweep beam leaves a scorched line.
- **Specimen One:** visible stitched seams and the collar numbers readable.

## Zion
- **Scrapper:** a spinning sawblade or spark wheel.
- **Rust Hulk:** smoke from an exhaust stack, flickering headlight.
- **Bolt Thrower:** a little crank arm that winds up.
- **The Crusher:** a big spinning crusher drum on one arm.
- **Gang:** bandanas and colours per member; Sparks leaves paint marks on the ground.

## Home
- **Snowmen:** stick-arm mittens, scarves, a carrot that wobbles.
- **The Snow King:** an icicle beard and a frost cape.
- **Worms:** shiny wet look and dirt crumbs where they come up.
- **The Fed One:** the gentlest; slow tears that drip, and soft glow pulses like breathing.

## Earth
- **Desert Stray:** mangy patches, one ear bent.
- **Cellar Rat:** whiskers and a pink nose twitch.
- **Night Manager:** a top hat, a name badge, and his shadow moving wrong.
- **Earth crew:** name tags and per-role props (doorman's gloves, airman's wings badge).

## Space
- **Belt Serpent:** glowing spots along the body, like deep-sea lights.
- **Void Moths:** dust trails that sparkle.
- **The Octopus:** suckers on the tentacle undersides and colour shifting when angry.

## Across all creatures
- **Hit feel:** a white flash on the body when hit, plus a squash on impact.
- **Shadow blobs** under every creature (cheaper than real shadows on phone).
- **Spawn and despawn:** dig out, drop in, fade in, by creature type.
