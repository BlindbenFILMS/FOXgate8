8 GATES · PLAYER PLANET · PLAN v1 · FOR BEN'S SIGN-OFF

Systems map

A lush tropical frontier world that the player names on first landing. It starts with no money, a Community Center with solar panels on the roof, and a handful of foxes. Nothing on this page is built yet.

1 · The build loop

1PLANPlanning Office board: pick a building. The board shows why it is needed (hungry, no power, no beds).
2BLUEPRINTAbout half the buildings need one: found in the world or bought.
3PLOTTap a glowing plot. 12 at start; clearing land unlocks more.
4GROUND WORKClear rocks and trees, level the ground. Needs the backhoe or other machines. Each job is a short minigame if you join in.
5MATERIALSTimber, stone, metal and crystal from the stockpile, gathered by workers or bought and shipped in (slow).
6CREWHire workers and a manager, or join the crew yourself to save money. Walking the site and helping speeds up the build.
7BUILDTimed in game hours (1 hour = 1 real minute). Queued builds keep going while you are off-world. Nothing new starts.
8UPKEEPEvery building needs workers, power and repairs after raids. It feeds back into needs.

2 · Resources

TIMBER · east jungle. Sawmill turns logs into planks.
STONE · from clearing rocks + the mountain path.
METAL · west crystal cave. Smelter (needs power) makes bars.
RAW CRYSTAL · west cave. The first income: sell it. The refinery turns it into power cells.
FOOD · farm (north), fish (sea), buffalo (plains). It spoils: see section 4.
WATER · a need. The river runs from the west mountain to the south beach. The water tower stores it.
POWER · see section 5.
GOLD · PLANET budget (starts at 0) and PERSONAL budget, kept separate. The player can donate to the planet, and the planet pays the player a weekly salary.

3 · Workers + the job board

Every fox has 1-2 skills and 1 trait. The JOB BOARD in the Planning Office has columns WOOD · FARM · FISH · HUNT · MINE · BUILD · GUARD, and you drag foxes between them. Worked example: moving everyone to WOOD to rush a building means no new food is made, and the stockpile starts to spoil.

CITY MANAGER = a recruited fox you hire. Their skills set how good their choices are. You can take over any part of the planet, for a short or long spell.

RECRUITING happens at other worlds and stations: pick foxes for their skills and traits. If you take too many from one world too fast, that world gets upset.

4 · Needs → happiness

FOOD · each food has a shelf life in days. Storage (SILO, STORE ROOM) slows spoiling, and the JERKY HUT and FISH SALTING HUT stop it. Farm produce spoils fastest.
WATER · river + WATER TOWER.
BEDS · cots in the Community Center → houses → apartments.
THINGS TO DO · tavern, park, market, school.
SAFETY · military law at the start, police later. Raids hurt happiness.

High happiness means new foxes ask to move in. Low happiness means foxes leave. With no amenities, nobody moves in.

5 · Power ladder

START · SOLAR ROOF
On the Community Center. Day only: at night the power is out, and foxes light candles and fires.
WINDMILL + WATER WHEEL
The wheel sits on the river and runs day and night. The windmill depends on the weather.
CRYSTAL POWER PLANT
Raw crystal → REFINERY → power cells → plant. Full power.

Needs power: refinery, smelter, clinic, school, defence turrets, space buildings, path lights at night, night work.

7 · Threats + the army

Bears come from the plains, pirates by sea or space, and the cave and jungle have their own creatures. Failure is HARD: raids damage buildings and they need repairing.

Phone controls: platoons follow you by default. Zoom out and tap a platoon badge, then tap the ground to send it. Platoons train at the barracks and get weapon upgrades. Galaxy law keeps the army on its own world. War is planned later with Ben.

9 · Four planets + rivals

There are 4 player planets: P1 north of Luxor, P2 west of Gaya, P3 south of Kufa, P4 east of Zion. Everything here is built once as a template and copied to all 4. Each planet has its own name, plots and save.

1 HUMAN

3 HUMANS
1 NPC rival
4 HUMANS

Rivals compete to sell crystal on one galaxy market (more sellers means a lower price), and each one defends its own world. A rival's brain is the City Manager running everything.

Raids + takeovers: rivals can raid each other and even take over each other's planets. Galactic law covers only the 9 worlds, whose leaders are sworn to peace and never attack first (unless the story says so). The 4 new leaders choose to stay INDEPENDENT or join the FOX GALAXY ALLIANCE.
