# NEBO — world brief (copied from 8GATES_DESIGN_BRIEF_ALL.md, main project)

## Nebo

**Style:** Green-and-gold forest world, an "N" crest on everything. Giant trees, mossy stone, hand-lit lanterns, round timber lodges, woven wood, bark mail, honey and mushrooms. Soft canopy light, dusk-leaning, quiet and a little uneasy.

**Story in one breath:** Nebo is a gentle woodland town under Elder Nova, and something is wrong in the forest: lanterns going dead, a ring drawn in the canopy, a logging gang (the Kindling) burning the wood, and a Hive that interrupted the transport. Wardens, rangers and heroes need you to clear the ramps, find Rooke and hold the Hive.

### Outdoors

- **NEBO TOWN SQUARE** (neboTownSquare) — lantern-lit crossroads plaza with TRANSPORT PAD at the centre (start), NS and EW avenues. Wilder (Trader), Brindle (Armorer), Marlow (Pet Store), Bracken, Angelica, Nettle, Sedge; strollers Rowan, Pica, Tarn, Aspen, Wren, Fable. Doors → Tavern, Item Shop, Armory, Candy Shop, Pet Store. Exits → Castle Path (N), Community Garden (S), Forest Path (E), Giant Tree Path (W).
- **THE HIVE** (neboHive) — BATTLE MAP. Huge open ground; defend the TRANSPORT PAD 30 seconds. About 12 enemies: 6 Hive Warden, 3 Hive Lancer, 3 Hive Drone. Exits → Town Square (N and S).
- **CASTLE PATH** (neboCastlePath) — organic stream-crossed approach to the castle. Gate guards Holt and Vetch at the arch, postman Pell. Exits → Throne Room (castle gate), Lodge (east gate), Nebo Town Square.
- **COMMUNITY GARDEN** (neboCommunityGarden) — allotment beds with an AMPHITHEATRE for the harvest play; WACK A MOLE pad (moles minigame); Garden Mole and The Molewarden fight here. Hazel (Garden Keeper), Marigold (Caller of the Play), dancers Bramble (Sun), Thistle (Rain), Quill (Earth), Pippin (Wind). Doors → Garden Farmhouse, Tool Shed. Exit → Nebo Town Square.
- **FOREST PATH** (neboForestPath) — woodland road with nine lanterns, four dead; RIVER BANK lookout; 3 hidden TRUFFLES. Fern (Road Ranger), 2 Sporelings. Door → Ranger's Cabin. Exits → Nebo Town Square, Forest Meadow.
- **FOREST MEADOW** (neboForestMeadow) — forest clearing with a quiet POND; more TRUFFLES. Teasel walks the edge. Glowspite x2, Sporeling, Thornback, Seedcaster, Rootwalker. Exits → Forest Path, Deep Woods, The Wick Fort.
- **DEEP WOODS** (neboDeepWoods) — dark forest, wide cut trail, smoke off the north-east spur. Thornback x3, Glowspite x2, Conethrower, Seedcaster, Rootwalker, Sporeling, boss THE DEADFALL. Door → The Tree House. Exit → Forest Meadow.
- **THE WICK FORT** (neboBanditFort) — logging camp of stumps and a central fire, called a fort; THRONE, BRAZIERS, CRATES, BARRELS, CAGE, BENCHES. Rooke (Tallyman, caged), boss Shrike the Snuffer, Kindling Faller, Stoker, Hauler, Bellows, Tally, Wick, plus 3 Roadman and 3 Slinger. Exit → Forest Meadow (east).
- **GIANT TREE PATH** (neboGiantTreePath) — warm roots at the foot of the giant tree, glider landing field. Bryn (Glider Crew). 12 moths, 2 Hornet, 2 Hornet Lancer. Exits → Nebo Town Square, The Climb.
- **THE CLIMB** (neboGiantTreeClimb) — inside the giant trunk, 8 RAMPS spiralling to the crown. 16 hornets, Hornet x3, Hornet Lancer, Glowspite x3, Sporeling x2, boss Hive Queen. Exits → Giant Tree Path, Tree Top.
- **TREE TOP** (neboGiantTreeTop) — the crown; LOOKOUT (the southern canopy, the ring) and FLY glider pad (glider minigame). Kestrel (Hero), Rill (Launch Marshal). Door → Lookout Hut. Exit → The Climb.

### Interiors

- **TAVERN** (neboTavern) — quiet, green-walled hall; SHUFFLEBOARD (minigame), DARTBOARD, 2 KEGS, bar counter (shop: stew, cordial, honeycake). Juniper (barkeep), Barley (Taverner), Linden (Hero, tracker). Door → Nebo Town Square.
- **ITEM SHOP** (neboItemShop) — lanterns moved to the front; counter shop (Heal Beam Battery, Energy Cell, Honeycomb). Wilder's shop. Door → Nebo Town Square.
- **ARMORY** (neboArmory) — green hall with COUNTER and BACK SHELF; gear shop (halberd, bark mail, longstaff, claws, maul). Brindle. Door → Nebo Town Square.
- **THE CANDY SHOP** (neboCandyShop) — warm sugar, copper pots, NOW HIRING card; CANDY SHIFT minigame. Angelica, Candy Maker. Door → Nebo Town Square.
- **THRONE ROOM** (neboThroneRoom) — grove apse with the Elder's THRONE. Elder Nova on the dais, Alder (Keeper of the List). Door → Castle Path.
- **LODGE** (neboLodge) — round-house lodge, trail counts on boards on every wall; SPAR ring. Warden Ash, Ivy (Drill Warden). Door → Castle Path.
- **RANGER'S CABIN** (neboRangerCabin) — trail maps and counts on the walls. Marten, Fallow. Door → Forest Path.
- **GARDEN FARMHOUSE** (neboFarmHouse) — seed, twine, ledgers; shop (poultice, cordial, seed cake). Rye, Farm Keeper. Door → Community Garden.
- **TOOL SHED** (neboToolShed) — tidiest building, every edge hung up; Hob sharpens blades. Door → Community Garden.
- **THE TREE HOUSE** (neboTreeHouse) — platform, bunk and pull-up ladder; treasury of the deep woods. Sorrel (Warden, North Spur). Door → Deep Woods.
- **LOOKOUT HUT** (neboLookoutHut) — canopy charts pinned up; CHART TABLE lookout (the ring measured twice) and landing book. Larkin. Door → Tree Top.

**Rooms:** 22 (the square lists a Pet Store door, but the game has no Pet Store room yet)
