## Jidda

**Style:** Bright lagoon archipelago by day: turquoise water as the road, boats and piers everywhere, sun-bleached stone and timber, salt-weathered shops, a fortress headland. Cyan-blue trims, lantern-warm interiors, surf wax and netting. Under the western Reach it turns dark, flooded rock and drowned walkways.

**Story in one breath:** Jidda sits on islands around a lagoon crest, and the water has been draining somewhere. Three torn pieces of a seed map lead you to a hoard on the Corsair Reach, where the floor gives way into a drain; down below the Jidda Crystal waits, one of the nine for King Might's space gate.

### Outdoors

- **JIDDA TOWN SQUARE** (jTown) — hub island in the lagoon with a crest mooring and transport pad at the centre; piers to every building. LONG BOARD shuffleboard marquee. Jamos (harbour board), Jorn (crest skiff), Jody, Jago, Jetsam, Jarl, Jib, Julep, plus 8 wandering townsfolk (Netter Pell, Cooper Wray, Marram, Brother Kelp, Bosun Tarn, Oarsman Dray, Sister Shoal, Fishwife Ivo). Piers → Tavern, Item Shop, Armory, Surf Shop, Smoothies. Exits → Castle Path (N), Farm Island (S), Surf Contest (E), Corsair Reach (W boat).
- **JIDDA CASTLE PATH** (jCastlePath) — the headland, fortress wall to wall, barracks isle off the east shore. Doors → The Castle (Throne Room), Barracks. Exit → Town Square.
- **JIDDA FARM ISLAND** (jFarm) — the only field on Jidda, carrot rows. WHACK A MOLE minigame (marquee, 20 carrots, one minute), MOLE HOLE. Jute, Jed, Jarrow, 4 Flamingos. Exit → Town Square. Mole hole from Mole Shaft comes up here.
- **JIDDA SURF CONTEST** (jSurfContest) — contest break, stands facing the water. THE BREAK surf minigame (three waves, one heat), heat sheet, five-board surf lineup. Job (champion), Joss, 3 Flamingos. Exit → Town Square.
- **CORSAIR REACH — LANDING** (jReachLanding) — pirate pier where boats tie up, bridges to three areas. Corsair Cutlass x5, Corsair Gunner x5, Flamingo x4. Exits → The Crag (N), The Shoals (S), Pirate Hideout (W), Town Square (E boat).
- **CORSAIR REACH — THE CRAG** (jReachCrag) — high ground of broken pillars and a cave mouth. Corsair Cutlass x6, Corsair Gunner x6, Flamingo x2, Sand Serpent. Exits → Landing, Pirate Hideout.
- **CORSAIR REACH — THE SHOALS** (jReachShoals) — shallow water, wreck on the bar, occupied watchtower. DIG SITE (reach hoard, 600 gold) where the spade goes through the floor. Flamingo x7, Corsair Gunner x5, Corsair Cutlass x4, Sand Serpent. Exits → Landing, Pirate Hideout.
- **CORSAIR REACH — PIRATE HIDEOUT** (jReachHideout) — stockade, gate facing the entry bridge. Corsair Cutlass x7, Corsair Gunner x6, Flamingo x2, Corsair Captain, Sand Serpent. Door → Corsair Hut (barred until the yard is cleared). Exits → Landing, The Crag, The Shoals.
- **THE SINK** (jSink) — BATTLE MAP, the landing fight: defend the transport pad through waves, put down all 4 GATES. Under the Reach, loot and the thing the pirates fed. Corsair Cutlass x3, Corsair Gunner x3, Corsair Captain x3, Sand Serpent x3. Exits → Town Square (N and S).
- **UNDER THE SHOALS — THE DROP** (jDrop) — dark flooded rock, no way back up. Sump Crab x6. Exit → The Flooded Gallery (E).
- **UNDER THE REACH — THE FLOODED GALLERY** (jGallery) — half-built walkways over water, crossed by RAFT. Sump Crab x6, Drowned Gunner x2, Drowned Corsair x2. Exits → The Drop, The Mole Shaft.
- **UNDER THE FARM — THE MOLE SHAFT** (jShaft) — one causeway over deep water. THE JIDDA CRYSTAL (relic, one of the nine) at the end, MOLE HOLE climb out. Sump Crab x7, The Drain Serpent (boss). Exits → Flooded Gallery, up to Farm Island.
