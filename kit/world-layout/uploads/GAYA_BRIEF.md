# GAYA (+ the Kyoto quarter) — from 8GATES_DESIGN_BRIEF_ALL.md

## Gaya

**Style:** Gold-trimmed stone-and-lantern hill town of a crystal-mining church order, lilac and violet banners, white sacred robes, warm lamps, steam and incense; cold blue crystal in the mountain and gorge. Then a second quarter, Kyoto: red lacquer and torii-red gates, tatami, paper lanterns, cherry-pink mountain road, samurai armour, sushi and tea, plus a moonlit kitsune wood.

**Story in one breath:** A transport landing is held at the Millstone, then the church of Gaya, which has dreamt of you for nine nights, sends you after the crystal vein in the mountain and the gorge's glass, and a sacred bath dream drops you into a kitsune wood where a samurai family's boy lands off a boat and flees to Kyoto, hunted by ninjas, with a castle plot, a champion uncle on the sumo clay, and an empress behind it all.

### Outdoors

- **GAYA TOWN SQUARE** (gayaTownSquare) — gold-trimmed plaza, the hub; TRANSPORT PAD at the crossing, STAGE, NOTICE BOARD, 4 fountain POOLS, NOW HIRING sign. Let (Warden of the Gate), Grish (Hero), Rill, plus 8 townsfolk (Plum, Mauve, Iris, Amethyst, Lilac, Orchid, Brother Cinnabar, Sister Damson). Doors → Church, Bathhouse, Tavern, Library (x2), Item Shop, Armory, Noodle Shop. Exits → Castle Path (N), Mountain Path (E), Meditation Canyon (S), Stadium Path (W).
- **THE MILLSTONE** (gayaMill) — BATTLE MAP: the landing fight. Snowy ring of spinning sand bands around a central CORE; hold the ground until the transport finishes crossing. 12 golems (Sink Sentries, Sink Chargers, 6 Sink Wardens), 1 TURRET, a BIKE and a TANK, ARSENAL. Exits → Town Square (transport pad).
- **CASTLE PATH** (gayaCastlePath) — approach lane with FOUNTAIN, SHRINE, crest medallion, two castle guards at the gate. Acolyte Bastion. Exits → Town Square, Throne Room (castle gate), Barracks.
- **STADIUM PATH** (gayaStadiumPath) — crowd approach to the tennis ground; USHERS, STALLS, SCOREBOARDS. Novice Pennant. Exits → Town Square, Stadium.
- **MOUNTAIN PATH** (gayaMountainPath) — church-ground trail; TRAILHEAD, SHRINES, CAIRNS, BENCHES, a stair up to a PLATFORM. White-robe check by Brace (novice), Warden, Tinder. 3 woken enemies later (2 Woken Crabs, Blue Shardthrower). Exits → Town Square, Mountain Hut, Mountain Base.
- **MOUNTAIN BASE** (gayaMountainBase) — "the cut face": cutters still working crystal; RIVER with a BRIDGE, SHRINE, SIGN, CAIRNS, CAVE MOUTH to the north. Quartz (Foreman of the Face). 10 golems (Crystal Crabs, Shardthrowers, Bank Golem, Road Warden) plus later Woken Crabs and a Vein Warden. Exits → Mountain Path, Mountain Cave.
- **MEDITATION CANYON** (gayaCanyon) — Gaya Gorge: rim, long BRIDGE, MEDITATION PAD with MATS and BRAZIERS, OVERLOOK with benches and the GLASS (LOOKOUT: look through live crystal to see Earth). Grand (Hero, commander), Fathom, Sounding. 14 BATS circle the bridge. Exits → Town Square.

- **EARTH** (earthCasino) — vision, no door: a Las Vegas casino seen from outside time; STAGE with MIC and SCREEN, ROULETTE WHEEL, CARD TABLE, DICE, SLOT MACHINES, BAR. The announcer, 2 dancers, a bishop at the cards, barman, ~10 gamblers; lit DOOR in the corner is the way out.
- **A WOOD, EARTH** (earthKitsune) — moonlit kitsune clearing "a long way before Gaya": central FIRE, STONE, wedding TABLE, CHEST (robe, stone, fan), lantern-lined PATH, BEACH and DOCK with 2 BOATS. Claude (ten-tailed head), Sedge, Ash, Sorrel-of-the-Stone, a light, 12 kitsune guards, the samurai family and the boy, a man in black. Gate → Kyoto Town Square.

### Kyoto: Outdoors

- **KYOTO TOWN SQUARE** (kyotoTownSquare) — red-lacquer city court, the hub; TRANSPORT PAD, STAGE, NOTICE BOARD, GO BOARD, tea mat, fox-mask barker. 6 samurai gate guards, monk at the temple step, geisha Suzu, tea master, go master, ~12 townsfolk. Doors → Temple, Onsen, Teahouse, Armourer, Library, Theatre, Sushi, Apartment. Exits → Castle Approach (N), Mountain Road (E), Sumo Approach (W), The Wood (S).
- **MOUNTAIN ROAD** (kyotoMountainPath) — cherry-pink road; ARCHERY RANGE with LINE and BUTTS (BOW minigame, master of the butts + 3 archers), TRAILHEAD, SHRINES, CAIRNS, stair to a PLATFORM. A pilgrim; 4 men in black, 3 dogs, 3 burning things. Exits → Kyoto Town Square, Sage's Hut, Under the Mountain.
- **UNDER THE MOUNTAIN** (kyotoMountainBase) — river valley; BRIDGE, SHRINE, SIGN, CAIRNS, cave mouth. 3 men in black, 2 dogs, 2 burning things. Exits → Mountain Road, The Hideout.
- **CASTLE APPROACH** (kyotoCastlePath) — red-gated avenue; FOUNTAIN, SHRINE, crest medallion. Samurai gate guard. Exits → Kyoto Town Square, Kyoto Castle, Samurai Barracks.
- **SUMO APPROACH** (kyotoSumoPath) — fan-crowded lane to the clay; GATE with name BOARD, STALLS, SCOREBOARDS. The champion signing at his gate, the gateman, a boy with a paper, a woman with two children, a tout, a rikishi's apprentice, samurai gate guard. Exits → Kyoto Town Square, The Dohyo.
