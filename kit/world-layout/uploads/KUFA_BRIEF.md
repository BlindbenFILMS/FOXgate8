# KUFA — world brief (copied from 8GATES_DESIGN_BRIEF_ALL.md, main project)

## Kufa

**Style:** Hot desert caravan town at the edge of the dry. Sandstone, brass over steel, canvas awnings, palms, camels and pack wagons; blazing sun, long shadows, the K crest set in the square. Water is currency: standpipes, jars, tally boards, one oasis, one cold room. Underground, a cold dry spy station with lasers and a green-then-red arch.

**Story in one breath:** Nine wells on the west road are failing in order, and the water broker's count does not add up. Escort the caravans west to the Way Station, break the siphon rig on the pipe head, then go under the oasis to Station 9 and face the Plated Coat (KASPER) at the Berth.

### Outdoors

- **KUFA TOWN SQUARE** (kufaTownSquare) — caravan gate at the crossing; K CREST in the middle, TALLY BOARD and water jars, running standpipe, TRANSPORT PAD, camels. Hadi, Zohra, Nasrin, Rafiq are in the shops. Sura and Jibril drink in the tavern; Jamil is in the creamery. Plated Coat and 4 Coat Guards appear late. Rukaya late. Doors → Tavern, Creamery, Armory, Item Shop (sign ITEMS), Spy Shop (sign SPY). Exits → Bazaar (west), Castle Path (north), Oasis (south), Stadium Path (east).
- **KUFA BAZAAR** (kufaBazaar) — souk of stalls and a dead-end CAMEL PEN with 3 loaded wagons; CARAVAN HIRING BOARD. Ghazi (caravan master), Qasim al-Warid (water broker), Ummi Reyhan (camel pen). Exits → Town Square (east), Caravan Road (west).
- **CASTLE PATH** (kufaCastlePath) — road up past a WADI and bridge, palms, lamps; barracks on one side, the hall at the top. Exits → Throne Room (castle gate), Barracks, Town Square.
- **OASIS** (kufaOasis) — the only standing water for two days; LAKE with a DOCK and SKIFF (boat), palm groves, reeds, fire ring, shelter. Bahri keeps it. Boat to the lake centre dives to Station 9. Exits → Town Square; boat → Station 9 The Lock.
- **STADIUM PATH** (kufaStadiumPath) — fork road with palms and booths, the stadium looming north. 4 Apron Scarabs, 2 Dry Devils, 2 Standpipe Husks, 2 Overflow Spitters. Exits → Town Square (west), Stadium (north gate).
- **KUFA STADIUM** (kufaStadium) — running track and soccer PITCH with the CISTERN HEAD ring at centre, GOALS, 4 drain mouths. Master Faruq runs the Watch; SOCCER shootout minigame. 2 Drain Husks, 2 Drain Spitters, 2 Sump Scarabs, The Cistern Wyrm (boss). Exit → Stadium Path.
- **CARAVAN ROAD** (kufaCaravanRoad1) — first leg, dry road with a MILESTONE, shoulders and ambush pockets; wagons roll when the road is clear. 6 Husks, 4 Grit Spitters, 2 Dune Scarabs, 2 Dust Devils. Exits → Bazaar (east), Far Leg (west).
- **CARAVAN ROAD, FAR LEG** (kufaCaravanRoad2) — the road that ate the last two caravans; CHOKE point and the pipe head RIG (THE SIPHON). Dahab hides here. 5 Husks, 4 Dune Scarabs, 3 Grey Frames, 2 Runners, The Breaker, Warden of the Siphon, other spitters. Exits → Caravan Road (east), Way Station (west).
- **THE WAY STATION** (kufaWayStation) — wall, gate, WELL and the cistern HEAD at the far end of the worst road. Rukaya (keeper), Bashir and his pipe crew. Exits → Far Leg (east), The Berth (north).
- **THE BERTH** (kufaBerth) — cut field, GANTRY, RAMP and a LANDER PAD in a red band; live cutter beams across the top strip. Nuri (weighbridge clerk), The Plated Coat. 4 Plated Frames, 2 Coat Guards, 2 Markers, The Factor's Frame. Exit → Way Station.
- **THE CONVOY** (kufaConvoy) — BATTLE MAP: defend the TRANSPORT PAD for 30 seconds while the interrupted transport finishes its crossing. 3 Road Spitters, 3 Siege Spitters, 3 Road Scarabs, 3 Convoy Wyrms. Exits → Town Square (north and south).

### Interiors

- **TAVERN** (kufaTavern) — warm hall where every caravan rumour arrives first; BAR (shop: harira, mint tea, pastry), JUKEBOX, DARTBOARD, FOOSBALL table (minigame), round tables. Nasrin the barkeep, Sura, Jibril. Door → Town Square.
- **ITEM SHOP** (kufaItemShop) — plain trader's hall; COUNTER (shop: Heal Beam Batteries, Energy Cells, dates, canteen), back shelf. Hadi the trader. Door → Town Square.
- **ARMORY** (kufaArmory) — hot forge hall, brass over steel; COUNTER (shop: halberd, brigandine, maul, spears, claws), back shelf. Zohra the armorer. Door → Town Square.
- **SPY SHOP** (kufaSpyShop) — dim front room, FALSE BOOKCASE hiding a BACK ROOM; COUNTER (shop: Signal Cell, Sealed Package). Rafiq. Door → Town Square.
- **THE CREAMERY** (kufaCreamery) — the only cold room on Kufa, mint and cream; ICE CREAM MACHINE with three nozzles (SOFT SERVE minigame), HIRING sign. Jamil. Door → Town Square.
- **THRONE ROOM** (kufaThroneRoom) — stone nave to a DAIS and THRONE, side aisles, TREASURY, crest medallion. Emir Tahir on his throne. Door → Castle Path.
- **BARRACKS** (kufaBarracks) — rotunda with ROTUNDA DESK, DORM aisle with beds, TRAINING stand, ARMORY stand. Captain Idris. Door → Castle Path.
- **STATION 9 — THE LOCK** (kufaSpyLock) — dry cold chamber, ARCH scanner (light not green), SHAFT up. Exits → The Halls, Up the shaft → Oasis.
- **STATION 9 — THE HALLS** (kufaSpyHalls) — corridor with floor MINES and LASER TRIP LINES. Exits → The Lock, Operations.
- **STATION 9 — OPERATIONS** (kufaSpyCore) — control floor, central TABLE. 4 Sentry Frames and Warden Frame. Nasr, Diya. Exits → Halls, The Ward, The Old Wing.
- **STATION 9 — THE WARD** (kufaSpyWard) — infirmary with a serviced TABLE and lamp, BEDSIDE. Kass lying wounded, Nasr, Diya. Exit → Operations.
- **STATION 9 — THE OLD WING** (kufaSpyWing) — store room with lamps, blankets, long TABLE, BOARD. Kass, Sahar, Tal, Ammar, Diya, Nasr. Exit → Operations.

**Rooms:** 23
