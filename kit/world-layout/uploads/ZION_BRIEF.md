# ZION — world brief (copied from 8GATES_DESIGN_BRIEF_ALL.md, main project)

## Zion

**Style:** Yellow and gold with a Z crest on everything: golden banners, brass, plank walls, cobblestone, dirt, rolling hills. Working frontier-industrial town of gold panners, monster trucks and oil derricks. Warm lantern light in interiors, bright dusty daylight outside; the Blackout map is pitch dark.

**Story in one breath:** Zada leads Zion (river rights, arena licence), and an oil derrick in the river's south-west corner is fouling the gold water; Clerk Pell sends you to wash a pan at the Deep Bend and bring the oiled pan to Zada, who caps the derrick. Meanwhile scrap machines infest the hill runs and a monster truck gang is dug in at the summit.

### Outdoors

- **ZION TOWN SQUARE** (zionTownSquare) — yellow-Z hub; round CREST PAD with FOUNTAIN at the crossing, rim lamps, flags, trees, planters, four avenues. Guards Halden, Vess, Corbin, Ryn; townsfolk Cass, Juno, Rell, Mika, Tobe, Wynn, Solen, Bram, Ivet, Dala, Kesh, Oro. Building doors: Tavern, Bakery, Armory, Item Shop, Pinball. Exits → Castle Path (N), Gold River (S), Hill Path (W), Truck Road (E).
- **CASTLE PATH** (zionCastlePath) — straight road north to the castle; DRY MOAT with STONE BRIDGE, two crest PLINTHS on the forecourt, CREST FORK. Guards Nesk, Dorrin at the castle gate. Exits → Throne Room (castle gate), Barracks (east branch), Town Square (S).
- **GOLD RIVER** (zionGoldRiver) — winding river with two bridges, shallows for panning, trees and rocks. 3 PANNING SPOTS (gold-panning minigame): THE NORTH BAR, THE MIDDLE REACH, THE DEEP BEND. SURVAY OFFICE (Clerk Wick, the register), CAMP SLUICE, ore cart, fire pits. OIL DRILL with derrick, two tanks, pipe rack in the south-west corner (the river quest). Enemies: 3 Scrapper, 2 Rust Hulk. Exit → Town Square (N).
- **HILL PATH** (zionHillPath) — rolling yellow hills; CREST FORK, 6 parked MONSTER TRUCKS on the road, HILLTOP OVERLOOK to the north (dead end), west LOOKOUT apron (dead end). Exits → Hill Run (north crest gate), Town Square (E).
- **HILL RUN** (zionHillRun1) — switchback trail with obstacles: BOULDER FIELD, MUD WALLOW, ROCK STAIR, RAVINE CROSSING, TIRE BERM; foot trail plus truck-only legs. Enemies: 3 Scrapper, 2 Rust Hulk, 1 Bolt Thrower. Exits → Hill Run, Upper (N), Hill Path (S).
- **HILL RUN, UPPER** (zionHillRun2) — steeper climb: LOG PILE, BOULDER CHOKE, MUD HOLE, ROCK SHELF, TIRE GAUNTLET; west LOOKOUT spur; summit pad is the MONSTER TRUCK GANG BASE (GARAGE with two roll-up bays, TIRE WALL, FUEL DRUMS, FLAG MAST, truck access only). Gang yard shootout minigame. Enemies: 3 Rust Hulk, 2 Scrapper, 1 Bolt Thrower. Exits → Gang Hideout (gang door), Hill Run (S).
- **TRUCK ROAD** (zionMonsterTruckPath) — cobblestone road with a CREST RISE forking north to the arena; TIRE STACKS, WRECK, OIL DRUMS, TRUCK STAGING YARD (dead end), arena forecourt apron. Zell the arena driver; Marn, Pell, Tarn; Guards Brac, Toft at the arena gate. Exits → Monster Arena (gate), Truck Shop, Town Square (W).
- **MONSTER ARENA** (zionMonsterArena) — big DIRT BOWL ringed by concourse stands, FLOODLIGHTS, FLAGS, CRUSHED CARS, JUMP RAMPS, central CREST MOUND, PIT with 2 parked trucks (FOXTRUCK CRUSH minigame, drive a truck). Ringmaster Vance. Enemies: The Crusher (boss), 2 Rust Hulk. Exit → Truck Road.
- **BLACKOUT** (zionBlackout) — BATTLE MAP. Pitch-dark ground; defend the TRANSPORT PAD and CORE at the centre for 30 seconds. 8 glowing PYLONS, a TURRET, parked BIKE, WALKER and TANK. Enemy gates: 8 Blackout Warden, 4 Blackout Sentry, 4 Blackout Charger. Exits → Town Square (N and S).

### Interiors

- **TAVERN** (zionTavern) — warm lantern-lit hall with crest runner carpet; BAR COUNTER with bottle shelf and 4 stools (shop), JUKEBOX, DARTBOARD, FOX HOOPS basketball cage (minigame), 2 booths, 1 round table. Barkeep Odessa; Zoran (Hero of Zion), Hoyt, Sable, Nel, Ida, Pross. Door → Town Square.
- **BAKERY** (zionBakery) — flour-dusted shop; OVENS, COOLING RACK, COUNTER, PASTRY CASE, DOUGH TUBS, FLOUR sacks, NOW HIRING sign; neon baking-bread sign outside. Baker Odette. Door → Town Square.
- **ARMORY** (zionArmory) — hot cellar smithy; WEAPON RACKS, ANVIL, WHETSTONE, SHIELD WALL, COUNTER (shop: Long Wrench, Plated Coveralls). Armorer Dorkin; Sten, Marrow, Corle. Door → Town Square.
- **ITEM SHOP** (zionShop) — shelves, CRATES, KEGS, DISPLAY CASE, COUNTER (shop: Heal Beam Battery, Energy Cell, Tin of Peaches). Trader Maren; Verna, Pip, Ordell. Door → Town Square.
- **PINBALL PARLOUR** (zionPinball) — arcade hall with neon pinball sign; 7 PINBALL TABLES (GOLD RIVER, THE CRUSHER, HILL RUN, BLACKOUT, TRUCK ROAD, THE ASSAY, ZION), CHANGE COUNTER (tokens), PRIZE SHELF, cabinet. Ripley runs it; Kell, Jonty, Ruse, Tolliver. Door → Town Square.
- **TRUCK SHOP** (zionTruckshop) — pit garage; one MONSTER TRUCK in repair (wheel off, jack stand), one on a LIFT, ENGINE HOIST, WORKBENCH, TIRE PILE and RACK, PARTS SHELF (shop: Shop Medkit, Pit Crew Vest). Bex; Gus, Tam, Halder. Door → Truck Road.
- **THRONE ROOM** (zionThroneRoom) — long NAVE with carpet and 5 pairs of pillars, 3-step DAIS, THRONE with spiked back wall, crest medallion, two GUARD ALCOVES, TREASURY with racks (river quest). Zada on her throne; Clerk Pell, Herald Ame, Guards Ansel and Rook. Door → Castle Path.
- **BARRACKS** (zionBarracks) — yellow stone keep in a zigzag: MESS with tables, BUNK wing (6 bunks), TRAINING hall with dummies and SPARRING CAGE (Fox Fighter minigame), WAR TABLE, VAULT with racks. Captain Boone, Sergeant Ovie, Trooper Bram, Quartermaster Fenn, Trooper Kiln. Door → Castle Path.
- **GANG HIDEOUT** (zionHideout) — plank-walled lair; THRONE OF TYRES on a dais, 2 FIRE BARRELS, TYRE piles, LONG TABLE, WORKBENCH, crates. Something in the chair is not asleep. Enemies: Ratchet (boss), Wrench, Sparks. Door → Hill Run, Upper (gang yard).
