## Luxor

**Style:** Red stone and basalt, ash and lava glow, L-crest banners, iron braziers and spikes; militant, hot, loud. Pyramid-and-keep architecture, bike tracks, boxing rings, perpetual ember-lit dusk.

**Story in one breath:** Lox's crystal has gone missing from the throne room, and the Pit boxers and the Run bikers each point away from themselves. Win both gangs' trust, clear the volcano and red plains, and find the truth buried on the plains.

### Outdoors

- **LUXOR TOWN SQUARE** (luxorTownSquare) — red-cobbled hub, wide crossing of four avenues; IRON TRANSPORT PAD at the center, clockwise BIKE TRACK ringing the square, bike racks, benches, lamps, CINDER CUP trophy stand. Vash on pad duty, Cinder, Beck, Rill, Cobb, Jax, Brek, riders Wick, Ansel, Peel, Sabe, Ornn. Exits → Castle Path (north), Volcano Plains (south), Pit Road (east), Zoo Path (west).
- **CASTLE PATH** (luxorCastlePath) — red road, tar-filled SPIKE DITCH crossed by an IRON BRIDGE, palisade, braziers and trophy racks. FORK with L-crest medallion. Vail on the north post, two guards. Exits → Town Square (south), Throne Room (north gate), Barracks (east gate).
- **PIT ROAD** (luxorFightingPitPath) — bike-lane road ending at a gated plaza; FIGHTING PIT sign, TICKET BOOTH (Tace keeps the card), guard posts, crates. Boxing Gym on the road (below). Tam sells water, Odile, Holt, Wren, Patch, Sela, Oddi, Ferro. Exits → Town Square (west), Fighting Pit (gate), Boxing Gym.
- **FIGHTING PIT** (luxorFightingPit) — round sand arena, BOXING RING in the center, four gates, 4 tiers of seating with 200 fox fans, braziers, IRON BELT trophy. BOXING minigame. Lyra the champion, Rell (sand marshal), Podd, Vek, Sura on the open card, Ferro. Exit → Pit Road.
- **VOLCANO PLAINS** (luxorVolcanoPlains) — ash fields around a big volcano cone, one LAVA RIVER with a LAVA BRIDGE, parked bikes. VOLCANO RUN bike race track (3 laps, lava jump). Orn the surveyor at the cave mouth, Tarn marshals, Lucius. Enemies: 3 Slagback, 2 Ember, 1 Ventmaw. Exits → Town Square (north), Volcano Cave (south).
- **RED PLAINS** (luxorRedPlains) — red river grassland, three bridges, roaming animals, road camp, west-flat clearing where a hidden ship sits. Dead end. Gault and Bel on road watch, Yuki. Enemies: 5 Ember, 3 Slagback, 1 Ventmaw, the Ashwalker boss. Exit → Zoo Path (east).
- **FURNACE** (luxorFurnace) — BATTLE MAP: huge scorched arena, defend the CORE and TRANSPORT PAD, 30 seconds, enemy gates. TANK, WALKER, BIKE, TURRET, steam vents. Enemies: Furnace Sentry, Charger, Wardens. Exits → Town Square (north and south).

### Interiors

- **TAVERN** (room inside luxorTownSquare) — warm red-lit hall; POOL TABLE (pool minigame, Dabb), BAR COUNTER (shop). Barkeep Vesta takes bets on the pit; Mell, Boge, Tib at the bar. Door → Town Square.
- **DELI** (room inside luxorTownSquare) — Foxy Deli, cooking-order minigame; counter run by Pia. Door → Town Square.
- **BIKE SHOP** (room inside luxorTownSquare) — TUNE PAD and bike counter. Sparrow builds bikes, Lucius behind the counter, Cobb tunes. Door → Town Square.
- **ITEM SHOP** (room inside luxorTownSquare) — potion stack and gear shelves, COUNTER. Keeper Ottoline, with Nore, Fig, Quill. Door → Town Square.
- **ARMORY** (room inside luxorTownSquare) — forge, grindstone, BLADE RACK, ARMOR STANDS (Pit Halberd, Basalt Plate, Vent Spear). Keeper Garrow, with Dace, Bryn, Halla. Door → Town Square.
- **BOXING GYM** (room inside luxorFightingPitPath) — heavy BAGS, SQUAT RACK, COUNTER, board of eight belts. Coach Bruno, Gid, Mox. Door → Pit Road.
- **THRONE ROOM** (luxorThroneRoom) — long red nave, colonnade, carpet, 3-step DAIS, spiked high-back THRONE with L crest, CRYSTAL STAND, TREASURY, guard alcoves. King Lox on his throne, Hest and Mord, Yuki. Exit → Castle Path.
- **BARRACKS** (luxorBarracks) — zigzag keep: MESS table for sixteen, BUNK WING, SPARRING CAGE, WAR TABLE, barred VAULT. Marshal Ede, Sergeant Nix, Fenn, Sark, Wick, Bray, Pell, Cass on the vault. Exit → Castle Path.
- **VOLCANO CAVE** (luxorVolcanoCave) — lava cave: STONE BRIDGE over a lava river, a hall, a LAKE SPAN over a lava lake, PRIZE CHAMBER with Kelm's LOST BIKE. Enemies: 3 Slagback, 3 Ember, 2 Ventmaw, boss The First Cinder. Exit → Volcano Plains.
