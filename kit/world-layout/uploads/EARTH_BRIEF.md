# EARTH — world brief (copied from 8GATES_DESIGN_BRIEF_ALL.md, main project)

## DIRECTION (Ben, s348)
- MODERN-DAY EARTH: a Nevada city of lights after dark. Modern buildings (glass casino towers, a black glass pyramid, motels, a movie palace, a diner, an airport), cars, neon.
- Earth has NO SPACE DOCK (its own transport). It has no king: CITY HALL (Mayor Mr. Win) is its "castle" with the helipad + teleport pad (→ Earth's transport). DRAFT for Ben.

## Earth

**Style:** Night-time Nevada boulevard: neon and marquee bulbs (pink, gold, cyan, red) against black desert sky; casino-carpet burgundy and green, brass lifts, oxblood lounge; cream-tile diner; dusty Mojave highway, chain-link and hangars; one white-lit lab. No space dock: Earth is reached by its own transport.

**Story in one breath:** The fox lands on a far-off Earth, the Strip after dark, and drifts through casino, mob and film-lot stories toward a sealed military gate in the desert, where a woman called Ilsa has been held for decades. Hold the landing, find the ring, open the gate.

- **THE HALL** (earthHall) — 200-yard corridor with the building's own defences, a room at the far end that is not empty. Exits → The Ring (N), Containment (S, sealed until the hall is cleared).
- **THE CAVE** (earthCave) — rubble-filled cave under a hundred tons of rock, the GATE glowing at the far end, breakable RUBBLE, a glowing PAD. Ilsa waits here (opens the ring for eight seconds). Pad → The Strip.
