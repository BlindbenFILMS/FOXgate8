## Home planet (named by the player)

**Style:** Cozy foxes' hometown with gold-trimmed signs, lantern light, lilac window boxes, white-robed church order, cold blue mountain and glassy lake. Mix of small-town square, arcade/clubhouse, and snow country.

**Story in one breath:** The player's home world, a town of foxes with a clubhouse of their own, pizza runs and sports. Down in the Glow-Worm Grotto a keeper couple fears the thing they fed for forty years; settle it, take the SEAM STONE to Hope at the castle, and gather the nine seams.

### Outdoors

- **TOWN SQUARE** (gayaTownSquare) — cross-shaped plaza, two avenues meeting at the crossing; TRANSPORT PAD at the center; building doors with signs. Townsfolk Plum, Mauve, Iris, Brother Cinnabar, Amethyst, Lilac, Sister Damson, Orchid walk the avenues. Doors → Clubhouse 8, Pizza Place, Tavern, Armory, Item Shop, Comics, Museum, Your Place. Exits → N Castle Path, E Ski Lodge, S Lake, W Stadium Path.
- **CASTLE PATH** (gayaCastlePath) — road to the castle; SHRINE with acolyte Bastion. Exits → Throne Room, Barracks, Town Square.
- **STADIUM PATH** (gayaStadiumPath) — road west; crest junction where Noble watches the games, novice Pennant. Exits → The Rink, Town Square.
- **SKI LODGE** (gayaMountainPath) — snowy lodge plaza; ski rentals by the shed, FIRE in the middle, CHAIR LIFT at the top. Nix runs the lift, Perri teaches beginners, Bram waxes boards. Pizza delivery stop. Exits → Lodge (Mountain Hut), The Slopes, Town Square.
- **MOUNT HOME PLANET SLOPES** (gayaMountainBase) — mountain with THREE SLALOM RUNS (Blue Ribbon, The Pines, one more), gates set, chair running, PATROL HUT, creek. Three ICE GATES spawn snowmen (Lower, Park, Far Seam; 240 HP each). Enemies: 3 Icicle Snowmen, 2 Snowball Sentries. Exits → Glow-Worm Grotto, The Snow Fort, Ski Lodge.
- **GLOW-WORM GROTTO** (gayaMountainCave) — glowing cave with boat water; the keepers' worm lair. Wren the keeper, Barrow the boatman holding the feed pail. Enemies: 12 Spitter Worms, 8 Burrower Worms, boss THE FED ONE. Pizza delivery stop. Exit → The Slopes.
- **THE SNOW FORT** (gayaSnowFort) — one way in, one way out, a trench-fort. Enemies: 17 Fort Sentries, 17 Fort Guards, boss THE SNOW KING. Exit → The Slopes.
- **HOME PLANET LAKE** (gayaCanyon) — still water, long JETTY and a PONTOON; DRONE RANGE (Lake 8 Range, three rounds: Sighting In, Pairs, Open Water), LOOK viewpoint. Gunner runs the range; anglers Ross (west shore), Brin (east shore), Otto (jetty), Mara (pontoon). Church readers Fathom and Sounding. Pizza delivery stop (Otto). Exit → Town Square.
- **THE GLASS** (gayaGlass) — BATTLE MAP: landing fight, defend the TRANSPORT CORE (center) until the crossing finishes; ARSENAL, 1 TURRET, TANK and WALKER vehicles. Enemies: 6 Sink Wardens, 3 Sink Sentries, 3 Sink Chargers. Exits → Town Square (pad), north and south.

### Interiors

- **CLUBHOUSE 8** (gayaChurch) — the player's own arcade clubhouse; 8 ARCADE CABINETS (4 each wall), COUNTER (run shifts: A Quiet Hour, The Evening Rush; token and item sales), wall BANNER and CREST, lounge, 8 lamps. Pip at front of house. Door → Town Square.
- **PIZZA PLACE** (gayaBathhouse) — Sal's two-oven kitchen; OVEN MINIGAME (Sal's Round, toss and top the pie; pepperoni, mushroom, olive, green pepper, onion). Sal; pizza DELIVERY runs start here. Door → Town Square.
- **TAVERN** (gayaTavern) — warm hall; BAR COUNTER with 5 stools (shop), AIR HOCKEY TABLE (ping pong table), JUKEBOX, DARTBOARD, 2 tables, 2 barrels, torches. Barkeep Sorrel, Grish the explorer, Volley and Pip at the table. Pizza delivery stop. Door → Town Square.
- **ARMORY** (gayaArmory) — forge-lit shop; COUNTER (Halberd, Reinforced Armor). Armorer Bree. Door → Town Square.
- **ITEM SHOP** (gayaItemShop) — tidy shelves; COUNTER (Heal Beam Battery, Energy Cell, Box of Chocolates, Keeper's Draught). Shopkeeper Fenn. Door → Town Square.
- **COMICS** (gayaLibrarySquare) — comic shop; two cardboard SUPERFOX and IRONFOX standees over SHELF SCREENS playing hero reels. Bramble the owner. Pizza delivery stop. Door → Town Square.
- **RELIC MUSEUM** (gayaMuseum) — round rotunda; 10 GLASS CASES for relics (2 tall by the standees), centre table, archivist desk, catalogue, lectern, 2 globes, 4 braziers, shelves. Quill the curator. Door → Town Square.
- **YOUR PLACE** (gayaApartment) — player's apartment; BED (rest), PLINTH and TROPHY wall, table, TV, couch, kitchen, window, posters. Door → Your Landing (Town Square).
- **MOUNTAIN HUT** (gayaMountainHut) — small refuge cabin with a HEARTH and kettle. Acolyte Ember-in-Ash. Door → Ski Lodge.
- **THRONE ROOM** (gayaThroneRoom) — castle hall; THRONE. King Grant, proudly average; Hope standing beside him (gathers the NINE SEAMS). Door → Castle Path.
- **BARRACKS** (gayaBarracks) — guard hall; Guard Captain, Master of Arms. Pizza delivery stop. Door → Castle Path.
- **THE RINK** (gayaStadium) — ice hockey stadium; ICE, nets, benches, tunnel; play 4-a-side vs The Cubs, The Pines, Iron Eight. Ref Furlong; skaters Fleet, Cinder, Bracken. Door → Stadium Path.
