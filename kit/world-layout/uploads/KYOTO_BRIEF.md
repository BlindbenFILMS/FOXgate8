### Kyoto: Outdoors

- **KYOTO TOWN SQUARE** (kyotoTownSquare) — red-lacquer city court, the hub; TRANSPORT PAD, STAGE, NOTICE BOARD, GO BOARD, tea mat, fox-mask barker. 6 samurai gate guards, monk at the temple step, geisha Suzu, tea master, go master, ~12 townsfolk. Doors → Temple, Onsen, Teahouse, Armourer, Library, Theatre, Sushi, Apartment. Exits → Castle Approach (N), Mountain Road (E), Sumo Approach (W), The Wood (S).
- **MOUNTAIN ROAD** (kyotoMountainPath) — cherry-pink road; ARCHERY RANGE with LINE and BUTTS (BOW minigame, master of the butts + 3 archers), TRAILHEAD, SHRINES, CAIRNS, stair to a PLATFORM. A pilgrim; 4 men in black, 3 dogs, 3 burning things. Exits → Kyoto Town Square, Sage's Hut, Under the Mountain.
- **UNDER THE MOUNTAIN** (kyotoMountainBase) — river valley; BRIDGE, SHRINE, SIGN, CAIRNS, cave mouth. 3 men in black, 2 dogs, 2 burning things. Exits → Mountain Road, The Hideout.
- **CASTLE APPROACH** (kyotoCastlePath) — red-gated avenue; FOUNTAIN, SHRINE, crest medallion. Samurai gate guard. Exits → Kyoto Town Square, Kyoto Castle, Samurai Barracks.
- **SUMO APPROACH** (kyotoSumoPath) — fan-crowded lane to the clay; GATE with name BOARD, STALLS, SCOREBOARDS. The champion signing at his gate, the gateman, a boy with a paper, a woman with two children, a tout, a rikishi's apprentice, samurai gate guard. Exits → Kyoto Town Square, The Dohyo.

### Kyoto: Interiors

- **TEMPLE** (kyotoTemple) — incense-filled hall; ALTAR in an APSE, PULPIT, CANDLES, BRAZIERS, CHANCEL. The abbot (keeps the door to the hot spring), 6 monks meditating, 2 acolyte posts. Door → Kyoto Town Square.
- **ONSEN** (kyotoOnsen) — steaming hot spring; RECEPTION, TOWELS, LOCKERS, GREAT POOL, PLUNGE, STEAM ROOM, BRAZIERS. Door → Kyoto Town Square.
- **TEAHOUSE** (kyotoTeahouse) — lantern-lit tea room; BAR counter, TEA minigame (tea master), GEISHA DANCE (Suzu), GO minigame (old master), JUKEBOX, PING-PONG, DARTBOARD. Door → Kyoto Town Square.
- **ARMOURER** (kyotoArmourer) — samurai smithy; COUNTER, FORGE, ANVIL, QUENCH, SHIELD WALL, ARMOR STANDS, DUMMIES, ORE CRATE. Door → Kyoto Town Square.
- **SUSHI** (kyotoMerchant) — ring-shaped SUSHI BAR under four lanterns, NOW HIRING sign (shift job pays for the room). Sushi chef inside the ring + 5 diners. Door → Kyoto Town Square.
- **LIBRARY** (kyotoLibrary) — "shelves to the roof"; six SCROLL ROWS (kitsune legends and more), CATALOGUE, LECTERN, GLOBES, BRAZIERS, STACKS. Door → Kyoto Town Square.
- **THEATRE** (kyotoTheatre) — rows of seats, CURTAIN, lanterns; NOH minigame "THE FOX WIFE" with three masks (wife, farmer, fox). The old actor + 5 audience members. Door → Kyoto Town Square.
- **APARTMENT** (kyotoApartment) — one rentable room; FUTON BED and CHEST. The landlady, Hiro (the boy, grown). Door → Kyoto Town Square.
- **THE SAGE'S HUT** (kyotoHut) — one room, one fire; HEARTH, KETTLE, TABLE, BUNKS, CHEST, WOODPILE. The sage of the mountain. Door → Mountain Road.
- **THE HIDEOUT** (kyotoCave) — long dark cave camp "somebody has been living down here"; WAR CHEST, mats, fire, TRAINING RING with DUMMIES, TARGETS, RACK, RIVER and LAKE with bridges, ROOF-FALL rock pile, glowing CRYSTAL, PLATFORM. 4 men in black, 2 dogs, boss ninja + big dog; the new empress, captive. Exit → Under the Mountain.
- **KYOTO CASTLE** (kyotoCastle) — tatami throne room; THRONE on a DAIS, red answer line, advisors, court benches, guard post. Emperor, general in red, new empress, girl with a tray, emperor's own guard, 4 northern guards, samurai gate guard. Door → Castle Approach.
- **SAMURAI BARRACKS** (kyotoBarracks) — armour racks, oil smell; DESK, rotunda, dorm aisle with BEDS, YARD with KENDO duel ring (vs the general's second). The general's second, 5 yard samurai, gate guard. Door → Castle Approach.
- **THE DOHYO** (kyotoSumo) — raked-sand clay circle with a rope RING; 6-bout SUMO ladder (Jonokuchi to Ozeki), benches, tunnel. Stablemaster, the champion (Yugga). Door → Sumo Approach.
