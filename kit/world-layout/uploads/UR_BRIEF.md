## Ur

**Style:** Orange terracotta and clay-brick city with a U crest on every banner and floor; sun-baked Sumerian-fox town round an old teleport pad. Brass tokens, lamp-lit tablet halls, a stepped pyramid with sphinx and monolith, reed marshes, one poisoned green lake and one clean blue one. Warm daylight, tense mood.

**Story in one breath:** Collared lab-mutant lizards ambush the square, and the trail (college, picket, lab, hunter's cabin, pyramid crystal) ends at the real cause: barrels of runoff dumped in the lake for four years. Clean the water, then escape the lab's test cage and leave from the pad.

- **LAB PATH** (urLabPath) — lab steps with a PICKET LINE: signs SAVE the ANIMALS, NO to POLLUTION. Protesters Nanshe, Kubaba, Lu-igi; lab liaison Ur-Ninurta. TOKEN. Exits → Lab, Clean Lake (south), Beast Run (E), Town Square (W).
