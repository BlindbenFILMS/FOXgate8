# MELEE WEAPON SOUND PROMPTS (s339)

One prompt per sound. Paste each into your sound generator (ElevenLabs Sound Effects or similar). Settings: duration about 1 s for hits and swings, about 1.5 s for draw and holster, prompt influence high. Make 3 takes of each and keep the best; the game can pick between takes at random.

File names: `audio/melee/<weapon>-<sound>.mp3`, for example `audio/melee/sword-hit-wood.mp3`. Keep the names exactly; the game will look for them.

The 9 sounds for each weapon: hit-cloth · hit-wood · hit-stone · hit-metalthick · hit-metalthin · hit-flesh · draw · holster · swing.
