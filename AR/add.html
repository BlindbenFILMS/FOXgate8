Add an artwork

Turn a photo of a painting into an AR target. Everything happens in this browser — nothing is uploaded.

1 Photo of the artwork

Choose photo
  • Shoot it straight on, evenly lit, with no glare or reflections.
  • Crop to the edges of the artwork itself — no wall or frame.
  • Busy, detailed, high-contrast art tracks best. Large plain areas track poorly.

2 Details and tap-to-hear spots

Click on the picture to add a spot. Visitors can tap that part of the painting to hear your words. Spots marked “glow” also shine in AR.

3 Build the AR target

4 Put it on the site

  1. Move both downloaded files into the AR\targets folder.
  2. Open AR\artworks.js in a text editor. Paste the block below where it says Paste new artworks here, with a comma after the } before it.
  3. In GitHub Desktop: commit, then Push origin. Give it a minute or two.
  4. Print the QR code next to the artwork.
