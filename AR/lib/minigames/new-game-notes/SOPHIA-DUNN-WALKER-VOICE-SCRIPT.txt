Save each line as its own mp3 with the file name shown, in AR/media/voice/  (for example crew-intro.mp3).
Say it just as written: the game matches the words exactly, so keep every word and number the same.

CREW CALL (Strengths & Limitations) - a film-set Simon Says          (prefix: crew-)

Intro
crew-intro          Crew call. Your crew is built into Sophia’s painting. The camera is on the left. The lights are up top. The sound is on the right. Each one has their own sound. Watch and listen to the crew, then play it back in the same order.

Start of each round
crew-round-1        Round 1. Watch and listen.
crew-round-2        Round 2. 3 this time.
crew-round-3        Round 3. 4 this time.
crew-round-4        Round 4. 5 this time.
crew-round-5        Round 5. 6 this time.
crew-round-6        Round 6. 7 this time.

Playing it back
crew-your-turn      Your turn.
crew-oops           Oops! Again.
crew-listen-again   Listen again.

Getting it right (say Action! like a director on set)
crew-action         Action!
crew-cut-5          Cut! Great take. 5 more.
crew-cut-4          Cut! Great take. 4 more.
crew-cut-3          Cut! Great take. 3 more.
crew-cut-2          Cut! Great take. 2 more.
crew-cut-1          Cut! Great take. 1 more.

The end
crew-wrap           Cut! That’s a wrap!
crew-end            One crew, one film.


ASK FIRST (Where People Are) - listen first, then help          (prefix: circle-)

Intro
circle-intro        Ask first. Sophia’s circle is full of people, and each one needs something different. When someone hums, tap them, or press Find them. Let them tell you what they need. Then give them exactly that.

Moving down the rings
circle-ring-edge    First, the edge.
circle-ring-outer   Now, the outer ring.
circle-ring-middle  Now, the middle ring.
circle-ring-inner   Now, the inner ring.
circle-ring-centre  Now, the centre.

The five people (these are the people in the painting talking: a different voice for each would be great, or friends and family)
circle-p1           I like it up here on my own. Just say hello.
circle-p2           Could I take your arm on these steps?
circle-p3           So many voices. Somewhere quiet, please.
circle-p4           Hi! I do not need anything. Just say hello.
circle-p5           Will you walk me to the very middle? I would love an arm.

Your answers (you, being kind)
circle-hello        Hello!
circle-quiet        Let us find a quiet spot.
circle-arm          Here is my arm.

How many are left
circle-4-more       4 more.
circle-3-more       3 more.
circle-2-more       2 more.
circle-1-more       1 more.

A wrong answer (said by the person, kindly)
circle-not-asked    That is not what I asked for.

The end
circle-all-heard    Everyone has been heard.
circle-end          You asked first.
