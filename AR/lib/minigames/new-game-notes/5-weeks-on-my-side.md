# 5 WEEKS ON MY SIDE (5-weeks-on-my-side): Hold Still
Made with Ben in the main chat, 5 Nov 2026. The last painting to get its own game: every painting now has one.
Files: AR/lib/minigames/weeks-still.js, AR/lib/minigames/weeks-things.js (no pictures: everything is drawn in code)
Export: holdStillGame
index.js line: '5-weeks-on-my-side': holdStillGame, // Hold Still: keep the gas bubble over the stitches in Ben's eye for 5 weeks of movies with friends; tilt or hold LEFT / RIGHT (Ben Fox)
voice.js PREFIX line: '5-weeks-on-my-side': 'weeks-',
Artist's clip at the finale: 3D/gallery/audio/art_0.mp3 (Ben, in his own words)
artworks.js: Ben's quote added to work.quote (it was empty).

How to play: keep the bubble over the stitches. If it drifts, tilt your phone, or hold left or right, to bring it back.
What it does: a round x-ray view opens over Ben's eye patch: the retina, the row of stitches, a safe band over them
and the gas bubble. Five weeks, five rounds. Each week a friend knocks (from the left or right) and brings a tape; the
TV in the painting glows and plays it as a quiet little loop (comedy, space, western, scary, the future). Hold the
bubble in the band to fill the week's gold ring; life nudges it (laughing, a friend sitting on the bed, popcorn, the
phone, a jump scare, a sneeze), harder each week. The bubble pings from where it is (higher and faster the further it
drifts) and the stitches creak as they strain; strain them too long and that week starts again (no game over). Each
finished week flies its tape onto the pile in front of the bed. Tilting can take the camera off the painting: the game
keeps going and shows the painting on screen. About 1 to 2 minutes.
Tested: the bot plays to the finale in about 70 s, no errors from this game; Ben's clip starts at the finale; an idle
player drifts, strains and repeats a week as expected; every sentence it says matches one of the 30 voice lines.
Recordings: none yet (ben-fox-VOICE-SCRIPT.txt has all 30). Until then the lines use the computer voice.
