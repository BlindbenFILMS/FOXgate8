VOICE LINES TO RECORD: ANTHONY FERRARO'S THREE GAMES (narrator lines for the AR games)
One Love · Light on the Other Side · The Button is to the Left

Save each line as its own mp3 with the file name shown, in AR/media/voice/  (e.g. AR/media/voice/love-intro-1.mp3).
One sentence per file. Say it just as written (the game matches the words exactly; punctuation doesn't matter).
Anything not recorded keeps using the computer voice, so you can record a few at a time.
Anthony's own words at the end of each game stay in his own voice (never re-recorded).

!! DO NOT RECORD "Wait." !!  In The Button is to the Left, the crossing button says "Wait… wait… wait…" in the
COMPUTER VOICE on purpose (like a real accessible crossing signal). Keep it that way. The game sends it straight to
the computer voice, so even a recording would not be used.

This list replaces the voice lists in the three merge notes (it splits a few longer lines into single sentences).


ONE LOVE  —  "Light up ONE LOVE"   (prefix love-, 19 lines)

Intro
love-intro-1      Light up One Love.
love-intro-2      The letters have gone dark.
love-intro-3      A bird lands on each letter and sings two notes.
love-intro-4      Then comes the drop.
love-intro-5      Tap anywhere on the drop to light the letter.

Each letter as it lights (say the letter name)
love-o            O.
love-n            N.
love-e            E.
love-one          One.
love-l            L.
love-v            V.
love-one-love     One Love!

Help and timing
love-listen       Listen for the drop, then tap.
love-wait         Wait for the drop.
love-early        A little early.
love-late         A little late.
love-help-1       The bird sings two notes.
love-help-2       Tap on the drop.

End
love-end          You lit up One Love.


LIGHT ON THE OTHER SIDE  —  "Keep rolling"   (prefix roll-, 19 lines)

Intro
roll-intro-1      Keep rolling.
roll-intro-2      Anthony is skating through the dark, toward the light.
roll-intro-3      Listen to his cane.
roll-intro-4      When it hits something, jump!
roll-around       Or move left or right to go around it.
roll-intro-5      If you fall, get back up and keep going.

Falling and getting back up
roll-up           Get back up.
roll-keep         Keep going.
roll-never        Giving up is not an option.

During the ride
roll-wait         Wait for the cane.
roll-flow         Flow!
roll-one          One more.
roll-dark-1       A dark wave is coming.
roll-dark-2       Another dark wave.
roll-listen       Listen to the cane.
roll-back         The light is back.
roll-catch        Tap to catch the light!

End
roll-made-it      You made it to the light.
roll-kept         He kept going.

(Also in this game: four short pieces of Anthony's own recording play mid-run. Nothing to record for those.)

button-intro-3    The crossing button is right next to you.
button-intro-4    Press it, then wait for the walk sign.
button-intro-5    Hold Walk to walk, and let go to stop.
button-intro-6    Listen out for cars!

Waiting and walking   ("Wait." is NOT recorded: it stays in the computer voice)
button-early      Wait for the walk sign!
button-walk-1     Walk sign is on.
button-walk-2     Hold Walk to cross.

The car that runs the red light

button-a-car      A car!
button-good       Good stop!
button-ran        That car ran the red light.
button-whoa       Whoa!
button-ok         Are you OK?
button-keep       Keep going.
button-waiting    The cars are waiting for you.

When you press Ask (the friendly passerby)

button-notyet     Not yet.
button-go         Go now.
button-almost     You are almost there.
button-stop       Stop!
