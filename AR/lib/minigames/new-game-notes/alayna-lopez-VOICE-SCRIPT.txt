VOICE LINES TO RECORD: ALAYNA LOPEZ (narrator lines for the AR games)

Save each line as its own mp3 with the file name shown (for example cane-intro-1.mp3), in AR/media/voice/.
One sentence per file. Say it just as written: the game matches the words exactly, so keep every word and
the ending (. ! ?) the same.
Anything not recorded keeps using the computer voice, so you can record a few at a time.
Leave a tiny bit of silence at the start and end of each clip; the game strings sentences together.
(Alayna's own quote at the end of each game is her own recording. Don't record it.)

3 games, 99 lines in all: Cane Beat 27, Pick Your Track 38, Still Me 34.

Intro
cane-intro-1     Cane Beat.
cane-intro-2     Alayna feels the city through her cane.
cane-intro-3     The stripes and the domes sound like music.
cane-intro-4     Her cane taps on every beat.
cane-intro-5     Tap with it, and the cane plays the song.
cane-intro-6     Six crossings.
cane-intro-7     Ready?

Each crossing begins
cane-num-1       Crossing 1.
cane-num-2       Crossing 2.
cane-num-3       Crossing 3.
cane-num-4       Crossing 4.
cane-num-5       Crossing 5.
cane-num-6       Crossing 6.
cane-last        The last crossing.

A new instrument joins the band (crossings 2 to 6)
cane-join-bass     The bass joins in.
cane-join-keys     The keys join in.
cane-join-horns    The horns join in.
cane-join-bongos   The bongos join in.
cane-join-strings  The strings join in.

She's across (one of these, by how many notes you played)
cane-across-great  The street is singing!
cane-across-good   Beautiful!
cane-across-keep   Keep tapping with the cane.

If you haven't tapped yet (once)
cane-tap         Tap on every beat.

The Help button
cane-help-1      Listen for the cane.
cane-help-2      Tap every time it touches the ground.

The end
cane-party       The whole street is singing!
cane-end         Your cane played the street's song!

track-intro-3    At every fork, two tracks call out.
track-intro-4    One says a wrong idea about blind people.
track-intro-5    One tells the truth.
track-intro-6    Steer onto the true track.
track-intro-7    Six forks.
track-intro-8    Ready?

Each fork begins
track-fork-1     Fork 1.
track-fork-2     Fork 2.
track-fork-3     Fork 3.
track-fork-4     Fork 4.
track-fork-5     Fork 5.
track-fork-6     Fork 6.

Which side (said before each billboard)
track-left       Left track.
track-right      Right track.
track-which      Which track is true?

The billboards: the wrong ideas (read them plainly, they're being shown up)
track-no-1       Blind people can't go out on their own.

track-no-4       Blind people have to give up the things they love.

track-yes-2      Lots of blind people see some light and color.

The Help button

track-help-2     Pick the track that tells the truth.

The end
track-party-1    The storm is over.
track-party-2    Alayna is herself again.
track-end        You rode all the way to the truth!

still-party-2    She was there all along.
still-end        Still Alayna, all along!
