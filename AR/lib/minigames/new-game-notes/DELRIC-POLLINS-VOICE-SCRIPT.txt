VOICE LINES TO RECORD: DELRIC POLLINS - THE THREE NEW ARTISTS WING GAMES
(Beauty in the Blurry · Everything in Its Place · The Temperature Setter)

Save each line as its own mp3 with the file name shown, in AR/media/voice/.
One sentence per file. Say it just as written (the game matches the words exactly).
Anything not recorded keeps using the computer voice, so you can record a few at a time.
(Delric's own quotes at the end of each game stay in his words, so they're not in this script.)

78 lines in all: 29 + 39 + 10.

BEAUTY IN THE BLURRY - "Bring the blur to life"   (29 lines)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Intro (calm, warm, a little hushed - the street is asleep)
blur-intro-1      Bring the blur to life.
blur-intro-2      Delric’s night street is asleep.
blur-intro-3      Drag your finger across the painting to wake it up.
blur-intro-4      When you are close to a sleeping light, you will hear it call.
blur-intro-5      Hold your finger there, and it wakes up.
blur-intro-6      Wake all 7 lights, and the whole street will sing.
blur-intro-7      You can also press Glow.

Each light as it wakes (bright, pleased)
blur-awake-neon       The neon sign is awake.
blur-awake-winl       The shop windows are awake.
blur-awake-lamps      The street lamps are awake.
blur-awake-sign       The billboard is awake.
blur-awake-cars       The cars are awake.
blur-awake-winr       The windows on the right are awake.
blur-awake-road       The wet road is awake.

How many are left (said right after the line above)
blur-more-6       6 more.
blur-more-5       5 more.
blur-more-4       4 more.
blur-more-3       3 more.
blur-more-2       2 more.
blur-more-1       1 more.

"Where next?" hints
blur-try-neon     Try on the left, up high.
blur-try-winl     Try on the left, in the middle.
blur-try-lamps    Try up top, a little left.
blur-try-sign     Try up top, in the middle.
blur-try-cars     Try in the middle, a little right.
blur-try-winr     Try on the right.
blur-try-road     Try down low.

The end
blur-alive        The whole street is alive.
blur-end-title    The street is alive.

EVERYTHING IN ITS PLACE - "Put it back"   (39 lines)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Intro
place-intro-1     Put it back.
place-intro-2     Everything on Delric’s desk has its place.
place-intro-3     When a wave knocks things into the sea, pick them up, and put each one back in its place.
place-intro-4     Tap a thing, then tap its place on the desk.
place-intro-5     Or press Pick up, and Help.

The desk, left to right (said slowly, one thing at a time - each thing lights up as you name it)
place-desk        This is Delric’s desk.
place-watch       The watch.
place-wallet      The wallet.
place-cologne     The cologne.
place-pencup      The pen cup.
place-glass       The glass.
place-books       The books.
   (These six are also used whenever you pick a thing up out of the sea.)

The waves (building up, a little urgent)
place-wave        Here comes a wave!
place-wave-2      Wave 2.
place-wave-3      Wave 3.
place-pick        Pick something up from the sea.

Picking up (the first time only)
place-where       Where does it go?
place-tap         Tap its place on the desk.

Putting it in the wrong place (friendly, not a telling-off)
place-not-watch     That’s the watch’s place.
place-not-wallet    That’s the wallet’s place.
place-not-cologne   That’s the cologne’s place.
place-not-pencup    That’s the pen cup’s place.
place-not-glass     That’s the glass’s place.
place-not-books     That’s the books’ place.

Help: where the thing you're holding goes
place-help-watch    It goes at the far left, next to the jar.
place-help-wallet   It goes between the watch and the cologne.
place-help-cologne  It goes between the wallet and the pen cup.
place-help-pencup   It goes between the cologne and the glass.
place-help-glass    It goes between the pen cup and the books.
place-help-books    It goes at the far right, after the glass.

Putting it back
place-back        Back in its place.
place-more-5      5 more.
place-more-4      4 more.
place-more-3      3 more.
place-more-2      2 more.
place-more-1      1 more.
   (Same words as the blur-more lines: you can record them once and save a copy under each name.)

The end of each wave, and the end of the game (calm, satisfied)
place-all         Everything is in its place.
place-calm        The sea is calm.
place-end-title   Everything in its place.

THE TEMPERATURE SETTER - "Hands up, hands down"   (10 lines)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Intro
heat-intro-1      Hands up, hands down.
heat-intro-2      You are Delric, on stage.
heat-intro-3      Listen for the call, then press hands up, or hands down, on the next beat.
heat-intro-4      The crowd moves with you.
heat-intro-5      Grow the crowd from 50 to 5,000.

The calls (HYPE MAN energy! Short and punchy - each one has to land in time with the beat,
so keep them tight: under about half a second, no breath before)
heat-up           Hands up!
heat-down         Hands down!
   (These two come up a LOT, maybe 15 to 20 times a game. Record a few takes and pick the punchiest.)

A wrong move (playful)
heat-other        Other way!

The end (go big!)
heat-5000         5,000 people!
heat-fire         The room is on fire!
   ("The room is on fire" is also the title on the end screen: one recording covers both.)
