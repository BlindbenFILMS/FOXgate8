VOICE LINES TO RECORD: RICKY RUZICKA'S THREE GAMES (narrator lines for the AR games)

Save each line as its own mp3 with the file name shown, in AR/media/voice/ (e.g. goal-intro-1.mp3).
One sentence per file. Say it just as written (the game matches the words exactly).
Anything not recorded keeps using the computer voice, so you can record a few at a time.
Ricky's quotes at the end of each game are NOT recorded: the games play Ricky's own voice.


GOALBALL IS... - "Block the shot"

Intro
goal-intro-1       Block the shot.
goal-intro-2       Goalball is played by ear.
goal-intro-3       The ball has bells inside.
goal-intro-4       Listen.
goal-intro-5       Is it coming from the left, the middle, or the right?
goal-intro-6       Tap that side to dive and block it.
goal-intro-7       Block ten shots!

Before the first throw (the referee)
goal-quiet         Quiet please.

The first three throws are called out
goal-from-left     From the left!
goal-from-middle   Down the middle!
goal-from-right    From the right!

A block
goal-block-1       Block!
goal-block-2       Great block!
goal-block-3       What a save!
goal-block-4       Stopped it!
goal-half          Halfway there!
goal-one-more      One more!
goal-ten           Ten blocks!
goal-star          You are a goalball star!

A goal against you
goal-goal          Goal.
goal-listen        Listen for the bells.

Finale
goal-end           What a game!


HOPE IS NOT LOST - "Fly to the light"

Intro
hope-intro-1       Fly to the light.
hope-intro-2       Storm clouds rumble ahead.
hope-intro-3       Somewhere else, the wind chimes of the dawn are ringing.
hope-intro-4       Hold left or right to steer the island toward the chimes, and fly around the storm.
hope-intro-5       Pass eight storms to reach the sunrise.

A storm appears
hope-storm-left    Storm on the left!
hope-storm-middle  Storm in the middle!
hope-storm-right   Storm on the right!
hope-follow-right  Follow the chimes to the right.
hope-follow-left   Follow the chimes to the left.

You fly around it
hope-pass-1        Through the storm!
hope-pass-2        The sky is brighter!
hope-pass-half     Halfway to the sunrise!
hope-pass-one      One more storm!
hope-pass-sun      The sun is rising!

You fly into it
hope-hit           The storm shook the island.
hope-follow        Follow the chimes.

Finale
hope-all-way       You flew all the way to the light!
hope-end           You reached the light!


SEEING THE WORLD TOGETHER - "Our song"

Intro
wife-intro-1       Our song.
wife-intro-2       She sings a little tune of low, middle and high notes.
wife-intro-3       Then it’s your turn.
wife-intro-4       Play them back in the same order.
wife-intro-5       Each tune gets one note longer, and every tune you finish lights up the city.
wife-intro-6       Eight tunes.

Turns (first tunes only)
wife-listen        Listen to her.
wife-your-turn     Your turn.

A tune played back together
wife-nice-1        Beautiful!
wife-nice-2        Together!
wife-nice-3        The city lights up!
wife-half          Halfway there!
wife-last          Last tune!
wife-complete      Our song is complete!
wife-longer        One more note.

A wrong note, or waiting too long
wife-again         Try again with her.
wife-listen-again  Listen again.

Finale
wife-world         You saw the world together!
wife-end           You made it together!
