VOICE LINES TO RECORD: LIVE THE BEAUTY (Deborah Tramentozzi) - "Breathe the Beauty"   (prefix breathe-, 25 lines)

Save each line as its own mp3 with the file name shown, in AR/media/voice/  (e.g. AR/media/voice/breathe-in.mp3).
One sentence per file. Say it just as written (the game matches the words exactly; punctuation doesn't matter).
Anything not recorded keeps using the computer voice, so you can record a few at a time.
Deborah's own words at the end (her clip and her quote) stay in her own voice (never re-recorded).

HOW TO SAY THEM: this is the calm, peaceful game. Slow, soft and warm, like a guided breathing exercise.
Leave a little breath of silence at the start and end of each file. "Breathe in." and "Breathe out." are heard
8 times each, so make them extra gentle.


Intro (played in order at the start)
breathe-intro-1      Breathe the beauty.
breathe-intro-2      Hold the button to breathe in.
breathe-intro-3      Let go to breathe out.
breathe-intro-4      Each breath wakes up the world around you.

Every breath
breathe-in           Breathe in.
breathe-out          Breathe out.
breathe-longer       A little longer.

Breath 1: the sun  (also says how to touch things)
breathe-sun-1        The warm sun.
breathe-sun-2        Feel it on your face.
breathe-touch        Touch it on the painting to play it.

Breath 2: the river
breathe-river-1      The river.
breathe-river-2      Hear it flowing.

Breath 3: the trees
breathe-wind-1       Wind in the trees.
breathe-wind-2       Hear the leaves.

Breath 4: the birds
breathe-birds-1      Birds above you.
breathe-birds-2      Hear them sing.

Breath 5: the bell
breathe-bell-1       The temple bell.
breathe-bell-2       Far away, on your left.

Breath 6: the flowers
breathe-flowers-1    Flowers.
breathe-flowers-2    Breathe in their scent.

Breath 7: the sky figures
breathe-song         The figures in the clouds are singing.

Breath 8: the light
breathe-light-1      The light opens.
breathe-light-2      You are inside the beauty.

The ending
breathe-deborah      Listen to Deborah.
breathe-end          You lived the beauty.

(If nobody presses for a while, the game repeats "Hold the button to breathe in." That uses breathe-intro-2,
so there's nothing extra to record.)
