GAYA · TENNIS OPEN · LAYOUT SKETCHES Three ways to play it on a phone Camera behind you, hard court in a big arena. Shots: flat hit, lob, smash, plus a serve power meter. Lessons first, then the tournament. All three use the real Meru HUD: the top banner (HP, EN, quest strip showing the score, clock), the joystick, the round 1 / 2 / 3 buttons with MENU in the middle, and the FOX SWAP / ITEM strip. What changes is how much you aim, run and time the hits yourself.
1aStandard HUD
The Meru button cluster exactly: 1 = HIT, 2 = LOB, 3 = SMASH (lights up only when the ball is high over you), the small middle button = MENU. You run with the stick, and the stick direction when you hit picks left, middle or right. Serve: hold 1, let go in the green.

GOODEasiest on a phone. You think about WHERE to hit, which is the fun part of tennis. RISKLess like the rest of the game's controls. Running is automatic, so skill comes from aim and timing.
1cTiming ring
You run with the stick. As the ball reaches you, a ring shrinks around it. Press 1 HIT, 2 LOB or 3 SMASH (same Meru buttons) while the ring is green for a strong, accurate shot. Early or late sends it wide. Serve: a bar across the bottom.
