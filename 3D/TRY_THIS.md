## What changed overnight (the hero pass)
Everything below is built to Meru's look: toon shading, ink outlines, warm lamps, dressed buildings.

- **Every world**: you arrive in orbit on that world's **space dock** (your ship, the gantry, the planet below), then teleport down to the crest pad. Board your ship to go back to orbit.
- **Towns**: paved squares, the 2D map's own lamps / benches / planters / crates / stalls / fountains in their places, real trees (palms with fronds, two-tier pines, round trees, cacti), grass and flowers, and buildings dressed per world (Gaya lavender stone + crystal church steeple, Nebo timber, Zion false-fronts and porches, Kufa adobe with domes and awnings, Ur terracotta with crenellations, Luxor red stone with columns, Jidda stilt huts, Earth neon).
- **A real castle at every Castle Path** in each world's style (Gaya's has its crystal; Kufa domes; Zion a timber fort; Luxor red stone; Nebo forest green).
- **Kyoto** (in Gaya) is its own walkable quarter: torii gates over the roads, pagoda, castle keep on the hill, the dohyo, stone lanterns, shoji doors, cherry trees and falling petals, a snowy peak on the skyline.
- **The Vegas vision** (in Gaya): sit on the meditation pad in the **Meditation Canyon** → the dream casino (LAS VEGAS stage, bar, roulette, blackjack, craps, slots — all playable) → the lit door → the wood → north to Kyoto.
- **Set pieces**: Ur's stepped pyramid and the glowing toxic lake; Luxor's volcano with the bike track and the boxing ring; Zion's gold river, bridges, oil derrick, camp and monster arena; Kufa's oasis and football stadium; Gaya's tennis stadium (Home: the ice rink); Earth's Ferris wheel, the Pyramid with its light beam, the casino tower, the welcome sign, and the Ring; Nebo's giant tree; Jidda's piers and the pirate ship.
- **Rooms**: churches have pews and stained glass, throne rooms a carpet and colonnade, armouries racks and mannequins, barracks bunks, libraries shelves and desks, labs glowing benches, shops shelves; walls cut away so you always see your fox.

## The second night: the hero pass on every world + the new space map
**Space map, rebuilt** (`space.html`, "Fly the Nine", or *Return to orbit* from any dock):
- The Nine laid out on the 2D map's 3x3 grid (Home · Luxor · Ur / Gaya · Meru · Zion / Nebo · Kufa · Jidda), Earth far off to the south-east.
- Every world keeps five installations, coloured in its own tint: **Space Dock** (land), **Mining Platform** (sell ore), **Shipyard** (5 upgrades x 3 levels), **Fuel Depot** (repairs + missiles), **Star Gate** (jump to any world or Earth). Deep Space Fox is the big ring station off Home.
- Planets have atmospheres, clouds, rings, moons and lights on the night side; a nebula sky; asteroid belts with gold-flecked ore rocks you can shoot open.
- **Corsairs** raid every 50-90 s from four ports in the deep (KRASS, GIBET, MURK, THRAX): attack runs, break-offs, jinking, gunships with spread shots, named aces. Lasers with aim assist and a lead pip, **homing missiles** (Q), lock-on (Tab). Fly to a port, knock out its four turrets and raze it: its raids stop for good.
- King Might's two machine cruisers patrol Meru (leave them be); haulers run the lanes.
- **Star chart** (M or the MAP button): tap a world or a port to set a course; an arrow points the way.
- Phone: left thumb flies where it points; FIRE / MISSILE / BOOST on the right; the red button docks.

**Every surface had a pass**, world by world:
- **Jidda**: road signs no longer swallow the camera; lighter crag.
- **Kufa**: a real bazaar (striped market tents with goods, rugs, lanterns, pennant strings).
- **Luxor**: the red river through the Red Plains with its three bridges; **ten zoo cages** (a lion, a bear, a stag and a serpent pacing; six empty, like the 2D); the **Fighting Pit** with tiered stands and a cheering crowd of foxes.
- **Nebo**: garden beds and a scarecrow; the **Wick Fort** palisade with watchtowers, gate and tents, and a stump field; **The Climb** between giant bark buttresses on plank ramps; **Deep Woods** in gloom with glowing mushrooms and a hollow log; **Tree Top** on a deck in the canopy with glider racks.
- **Ur**: Hunters Pond (pond, dock, log cabin, rowboat, drying racks); the Beast Run's rusted open pens; Clean Lake's shore and jetty.
- **Zion**: Gold River is one smooth river now; arena bleachers full of cheering foxes.
- **Home**: snow on the Ski Lodge, Slopes, Snow Fort and Rink zones (frosted pines, falling snow), a chairlift that runs, a bonfire, snowmen, ski gates, the Snow Fort's walls, towers and ice throne; the Lake is a lake.
- **Earth**: brighter neon nights, asphalt roads, the **Starlite drive-in** (a screen that plays, classic cars, speaker posts), **Fremont's LED canopy** cycling colours, the neon boneyard and cowboy, the Mojave gas station, the Compound's hangars and tanker, Speedway stands.
- Everywhere: the camera lifts over roofs instead of burying itself in walls, arrivals turn you to a clear view, fewer and better-spaced lamps, buildings that were missing on open ground now stand.
