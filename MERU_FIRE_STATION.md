**3 · CAT STUCK IN A TREE**
- CHIEF: Cat in a tree by the gardens. Bring the short ladder and some patience.
- RUNG (tip): Don't grab. Hold out a paw and wait.
- CHIEF (done): The cat's fine. The cat is always fine.

**4 · DRIVE THE FIRE TRUCK TO A CALL**
- AXLE: Call came in! You drive, I'll work the siren. Follow the red arrow.
