8 GATES - MERU (FRESH START)

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (do not run it from inside the zip).
2. Double-click PLAY-MERU.bat
   Your browser opens http://localhost:8088/FOXGATE8-FRESH.dc.html
3. Tap PLAY - SOUND ON.

Needs Python (python.org, tick "Add to PATH") or Node (nodejs.org).
Keep the black window open while you play; close it to stop.

Other pages work the same way, e.g.
http://localhost:8088/FOXGATE8-PLAYABLE.dc.html?start=voices
