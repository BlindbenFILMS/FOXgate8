## The map at game start (Meru-sized, almost empty)
- CENTRE: COMMUNITY CENTER. Main room: kitchen + community table, an item shop counter, the CITY PLANNING OFFICE at the front. A little APARTMENT for the player. At night a few foxes set up COTS in the main room.
- A SPACESHIP LANDING PAD near the Community Center, joined by a BRICK PATH.
- SOUTH: a beach, the open ocean (swimmable; fish to catch). 3 ISLANDS big enough to walk round and build on (reach by RAFT first, BOAT later).
- WEST: up a mountain path, the CRYSTAL CAVE (being mined): RAW CRYSTAL (the rarest, most valuable thing in the galaxy: power or sell) + building METALS. The cave branches in every direction: bats, crystal golems, big cave rats.
- EAST: a deep thick forest (walkable), creatures in it, trees to cut for timber.
- NORTH: THE FARM (the only one, feeds the whole planet): fields, farmhouse / bunkhouse where its foxes work + sleep, a barn with animals.
- FURTHER NORTH: great open plains with buffalo (huntable for food) and bears (sometimes attack the city centre).

## Systems (Ben's numbered list, grouped)
1. BUILD CHOICES: what to build, when, and why.
2. LABOUR: hire workers; assign a manager OR join the crew yourself (saves money) and manage them. Only so many workers at a time.
3. MATERIALS: find / source them (timber from the forest, metals + crystal from the mine, buy the rest).
4. BLUEPRINTS: some buildings need one, found or bought.
5. GROUND WORK: clear rocks / trees, level the ground; needs heavy machinery (backhoe etc.).
6. SITES: the player picks the spots (keep it simple).
7. NEEDS: enough farms to feed everyone + the buildings people need to thrive (housing, amenities, things to do). Unhappy foxes move away; no amenities = nobody moves in.
8. (Ben's "0") RECRUITING: foxes from other planets + stations, chosen for skills + good qualities. Other planets get upset if you take too many too fast.
9. DEFENCE: planetary defences against pirate raids.
10. SPACE: shipyard, spacedock, space mine at the asteroid belt, a SPACE STATION, fuel yard. NO stargate (cannot be built).
- TIME passes while you are on the planet; builds run on a schedule; resources are limited.
- CITY MANAGER (optional play): makes the choices for you; the planet improves each visit; the player can take over any part for short or long spells.
- WORK TASKS: workers assigned to tasks work on their own; resources pile up for use.
- MONEY: a PLANETARY BUDGET and a PERSONAL budget, separate; the player can DONATE to the planet; the planet pays the player a WEEKLY SALARY.
- LAW + MILITARY: at start there are no police, only MILITARY LAW; a police station gets built later. The military trains, gets simple upgrades (buy better weapons for a platoon). If pirates or bears breach, the player directs a PLATOON as one unit; several platoons in the army. GALAXY LAW: no planet may move its army into another planet's territory, so armies stay on world unless at war (war + armies: later, with Ben).

## DECISIONS (Ben, s497 planning pass — do not re-ask). Full page: Player Planet Systems Map.dc.html
- Name: the PLAYER names it on first landing. Look: LUSH TROPICAL (jungle east, white beach, turquoise sea).
- Sites: FIXED BUILD PLOTS (glowing pads, tap one); ~12 at start, more unlock as land is cleared.
- Blueprints: about half (anything past basics). Building list v1 = every option offered (town, industry, defence, space) + SILO, STORE ROOM, JERKY HUT, FISH SALTING HUT, WATER TOWER, LIGHTHOUSE, WINDMILL, WATER WHEEL, CRYSTAL POWER PLANT.
- Join the crew: minigame per ground job + walk-and-help on building sites.
- Time: 1 game hour = 1 real minute (day = 24 min). Away: only queued builds finish, nothing new starts.
- City Manager = a fox you hire from recruits (skills matter).
- Fail: HARD — raids damage buildings, repairs needed.
- Workers: JOB BOARD, drag foxes between jobs (WOOD/FARM/FISH/HUNT/MINE/BUILD/GUARD). Shifting all to wood = no new food.
- Food spoils: shelf life per food; storage slows it; jerky/salting stop it; farm goods spoil fastest.
- Water is a NEED. River from the west mountain to the south beach (water wheel on it).
- Lighthouse: safer night sailing to islands + warns of sea raids.
- Power: start SOLAR on the Community Center roof (out at night → candles + fires) → WINDMILL + WATER WHEEL → CRYSTAL POWER PLANT (raw crystal → refinery → plant). Needs power: refinery, smelter, clinic, school, turrets, space buildings, path lights, night work.
- Money: planet starts BROKE; first income = selling crystal.
- Military: platoons follow by default; zoom out → tap badge → tap ground to send.
